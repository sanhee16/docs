Support

We are here to help with Logic Game.

Contact us

Please email support@logicgame.app. Include your device model, OS version, app version, and a short description of the issue. Screenshots are helpful when available.

Account and data requests

You can delete your account from the App’s settings. For help with account access, data deletion, or another privacy request, contact us at the email above using the email address associated with your account.

Common questions

My progress is missing. Guest progress is stored on the device. Sign in to synchronize eligible progress across devices.

I did not receive a reward after an ad. Please send the approximate time of the issue, your device model, and app version so we can investigate.

How do I change language? Open Settings in the App and select Korean or English.

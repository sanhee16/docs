Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

Logic Game (the “App”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information the App processes and why.

Information we process

  • Account information: if you create or sign in to an account, we process your email address, nickname, account identifier, and the authentication information provided by your chosen sign-in provider (email, Google, or Apple).
  • Game data: the App stores your game progress, records, settings, language preference, and hint or ticket status. This information is kept on your device. When you are signed in, relevant progress may also be synchronized with our backend so it can be restored on another device.
  • Advertising data: if you choose to view a rewarded advertisement, Google Mobile Ads may collect and process information as described in Google’s privacy policies. The App uses rewarded ads to provide optional in-game rewards.
  • Support communications: if you contact us, we process the information you include in your message in order to respond and resolve the request.

How we use information

We use information to provide and maintain the App, authenticate your account, save or synchronize your progress, provide optional rewarded ads, respond to support requests, and protect the App from misuse.

Service providers

We use Supabase to provide account authentication and cloud data services. We may use Google Sign-In, Sign in with Apple, and Google Mobile Ads when those features are available. These providers process information under their own privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Local game data remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the App. You can delete your account from the App’s settings; this requests deletion of the associated account and cloud data. We retain support messages only as long as needed to handle the request and meet legal obligations.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age at which consent is required in their country. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. We will publish the updated version on this page and revise the effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please contact us through the Support page.
